On & Off The Pitch

OUR APPROACH
GOES BEYOND
FOOTBALL.

Placing equal importance on character, discipline, leadership, personal growth and faith in God. We believe true greatness is built from the inside out.

"Character & Integrity In Every Action"

Football is a mirror of life. How an athlete responds to a referee’s whistle, a mistake, or a teammate’s struggle reveals their inner character. We instill accountability, honesty, and sportsmanship so our players command respect everywhere they go.

• Pillar 01: Character Development
"Discipline & Relentless Personal Growth"

Talent without discipline is wasted potential. Through structured coaching and high standards at Thatchfield grounds, players learn that self-control, punctual habits, and consistent effort are the keys to breakthrough both academically and athletically.

• Pillar 02: Discipline & Self-Mastery
"Leadership & Elevating Those Around You"

We raise athletes who do not shrink from responsibility. Our mentorship instills voice, empathy, and positive influence, empowering young footballers to be role models in their schools, families, and communities.

• Pillar 03: Purposeful Leadership
"Confidence & Unshakable Faith in God"

When players understand who they are and recognize their God-given gifts, fear of failure dissolves. We nurture a deep-rooted spiritual foundation that gives young athletes peace under pressure and courage to play beautiful.

• Pillar 04: Faith in God & Core Confidence

Everything You Need To Know

FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS.

What makes Jonga Bonita different from regular football clubs? +
We are a football development and mentorship organisation. While we train players to be technically gifted and tactically intelligent, our approach places equal weight on character, discipline, leadership, and faith in God. We develop the complete individual on and off the pitch.
Where are your coaching sessions and camps held? +
All training programmes, individual coaching, and holiday football camps take place at our base: Thatchfield Sports Centre, Brakfontein Rd, Centurion, GP, South Africa.
What age groups and skill levels do you mentor? +
We work with young footballers across grassroots discovery (ages 7-11), competitive youth squads (ages 12-16), and senior pathway players (ages 17+), offering both individual 1-on-1 coaching and structured group development programmes.
How does faith in God integrate into training? +
Faith is not treated as an afterthought; it is our foundation. We teach players to honor God with their work ethic, treat others with love and respect, play without fear, and understand their identity beyond sports statistics.
